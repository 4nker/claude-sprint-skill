# Working rules on every project

- Speak plainly: the plain-reply rules apply to every reply, always, without being called.
- At the start of every session in a project, the sprint skill runs by itself. It looks for `docs/BRIEF.md`; if the project has none it asks once whether to work with a brief and sprints or as a quick job, creates the four standard documents either way (brief, sprint plan, updates, decisions), and records the answer in the brief. The plan is the truth about progress, not memory.
- New discoveries go in `docs/UPDATES.md` and owner decisions in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, in the same session.
- Open decisions are asked of the owner, not guessed. Where a plan gives a default, use it and say so.
